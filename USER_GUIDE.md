# TimeBank User Guide

Welcome to TimeBank! This guide will help you navigate the platform whether you are here to learn, teach, or both.

## 🏁 Getting Started

### 1. Registration
When you sign up, you must choose a role:
- **Learner**: Focused on enrolling in courses and gaining skills.
- **Instructor**: Focused on creating content and sharing knowledge.
- *Note: Every new user is gifted **50 Time Coins** to start their journey.*

### 2. The Dashboard
Your personal dashboard shows:
- Your current **Time Coin balance**.
- Your active enrollments (if you're a Learner).
- Your created courses and pending student requests (if you're an Instructor).

---

## 🎓 For Learners

### Finding a Course
1. Browse the [Marketplace](http://127.0.0.1:8000/courses/).
2. Filter by categories like **Programming**, **Design**, or **Fitness**.
3. Click on a course to view details, cost, and duration.

### Enrolling
1. Click the **Enroll Now** button on a course page.
2. If you have enough coins, a request will be sent to the Instructor.
3. Once the Instructor **approves** your request, the coins will be deducted from your wallet, and you will gain full access to the course materials.

---

## 👨‍🏫 For Instructors

### Creating a Course
1. Go to your dashboard and click **Create New Course**.
2. Provide a title, engaging description, and select a category.
3. Set a **Cost** in Time Coins (keep it fair to attract learners!).
4. Add a YouTube link for video content and any additional notes.

### Managing Students
1. You will receive notifications for new enrollment requests.
2. Review the requests on your dashboard.
3. **Approve** a request to welcome a student and earn Time Coins.

---

## 💰 The Wallet System
- **Your Balance**: Can be viewed at the top of every page.
- **Earning Coins**: The only way to earn more coins is by becoming an Instructor and teaching others!
- **Spending Coins**: Coins are spent only when an enrollment request is approved.

## 🛠 Troubleshooting
- **Login Issues**: Ensure your username and password are correct.
- **Insufficient Coins**: If you run out of coins, consider creating a course to share a skill you already have!
- **Video Not Loading**: Ensure the YouTube link provided by the instructor is valid.
